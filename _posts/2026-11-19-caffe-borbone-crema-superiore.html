Back to TK Maxx for this one. Whenever I'm in there I always check the coffee section, and this time a bright blue bag from Naples was sat on the shelf for £5.99.

The bag says "Loved by Italians", so I thought I would see if it's loved by a Welshman too.

The Coffee

Caffè Borbone are from Napoli and the Crema Superiore is roasted in Italy. It's an Arabica and Robusta blend (no percentages given), and the bag calls it a medium dark roast with an intensity of 7 out of 10. The only tasting note on the front is "Rich & Smooth".

KaffeK describe it as "refined, creamy, and has a touch of sweetness" and pitch it at lattes and cappuccinos. I paid £5.99 for a 500g bag of whole beans in TK Maxx.

Setup

Ninja Luxe Café Pro ES701UK on Cold Press Espresso, double shot, with the strength turned all the way up (3 dots out of 3). The Ninja recommended a grind size of 12 and I stuck with it.

The shot went straight into my Stanley tumbler, then I filled it up three quarters of the way with cold tap water.

The Taste

Black first, as always. It was strong and quite bold, and not very sweet at all. The crema leaves a bit of flavour on your tongue after each sip.

Then I added just a splash of milk, and that improved the whole cup. It tasted better overall and a tad sweeter (which makes sense, given they pitch it at milky drinks).

Considerations

Price wise, it's hard to beat. £5.99 for 500g works out at £11.98 a kilo, which is the cheapest bag on the blog so far (Caffé Prima was £17.49 a kilo before delivery). TK Maxx stock comes and goes though, so if it's not on the shelf, KaffeK sell it online with free delivery over £39.

If you're after something sweet or fruity to drink black, this isn't the one for you. It's a strong Italian style blend and it's at its best with a bit of milk.

The Score

All in all, it was a decent cup, definitely better with a bit of milk, and a good strong wake up call for the morning.

Overall, I will give it 3 beans out of 5.
